How to score higher

  • Aim for 110–150 words per minute. That is about 85–110 words in 45 seconds.
  • Answer first, then explain. “I prefer X because…” in your first sentence.
  • Give one specific example. A name, a place, a time: “Last semester, my roommate…”
  • Use linking words: because, for example, in addition, however, as a result, that's why.
  • Cut fillers. Replace “um / uh” with a short silent pause.
  • Keep talking until the timer ends. Finishing early costs points for development.
